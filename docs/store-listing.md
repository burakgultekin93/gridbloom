## English

**App name (30):** Blazegrid: Ice & Fire Puzzle

**Subtitle (30, App Store):** Place blocks, set lines ablaze

**Short description (80):** Place blocks, blast lines and heat your streak from ice to fire!

**Full description:**

Blazegrid is a block puzzle that runs from ice to fire. Place pieces on an 8x8 board and blast full rows and columns off the board.

- While your streak is cold, cleared lines shatter like ice. As it heats up, they explode into fireballs.
- Keep clearing and the screen warms from ice to fire. Keep the streak alive and watch the flames grow.
- Clear the whole board for an ice-and-fire blast, and the blocks change look: tiles, crystal gems, ocean bubbles, autumn leaves.
- Put your own photo or one of 12 ready-made pictures on the board and reveal it as you place blocks.
- Choose from 6 block patterns.
- No timer. Stop any time and pick up where you left off.
- Works offline. No account needed.
