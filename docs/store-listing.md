## English

**App name (30):** Gridbloom: Block Puzzle

**Short description (80):** Place blocks, clear lines, heat up your streak and make your garden bloom!

**Full description:**

Gridbloom is a calm but gripping block puzzle. Place pieces on an 8x8 board and clear full rows and columns as they burst into flowers.

- Keep clearing and the screen warms from ice to fire. Keep the streak alive and watch the flames grow.
- Clear the whole board and the blocks change look: garden tiles, crystal gems, ocean bubbles, autumn leaves.
- Put your own photo or one of 12 ready-made pictures on the board and reveal it as you place blocks.
- Choose from 6 block patterns.
- No timer. Stop any time and pick up where you left off.
- Works offline. No account needed.
