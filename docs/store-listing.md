## English (U.S.), primary

The listing leads with Heat mode and avoids "blast" and similar words, so the
store page does not read as one more copy of other block games (guideline 4.3).

**Name (30):** Blazegrid: Block Puzzle

**Subtitle (30):** Melt ice, put out the fire

**Promotional text (170):** Heat mode: frozen cells lock your board and fire spreads from block to block. Clear lines to melt the ice and put out the flames before they take over.

**Description:**

Blazegrid is a block puzzle where the board fights back.

HEAT MODE
- Frozen cells lock parts of the board. Clear the lines around them to crack and melt the ice.
- Every few moves a hazard wave arrives: new ice, or a fire that catches one of your blocks.
- A burning block counts down, then the fire jumps to its neighbour. Clear its line in time to put it out.
- Fire that jumps onto ice dies out in steam, so ice can be your shield.
- Heat mode has its own goals: thaw the ice, douse the fires, keep your streak.

CLASSIC MODE
- Place pieces on an 8x8 board and clear full rows and columns.
- Your streak heats the screen from ice to fire. Clear the whole board and your blocks switch to a new style: Neon, Ice, Magma or Crystal.
- Mini goals in every game give bonus points, and a record bar shows how close you are to your best.

ALSO
- Put your own photo or one of 12 ready-made pictures on the board and reveal it as you place blocks.
- 6 block patterns and 3 sound packs: Classic, Arcade and Pop.
- No timer. Stop any time and pick up where you left off.
- Works offline. No account needed.
