## Notes (App Review Information → Notes)

Thank you for your earlier review. Since then the app has been substantially redesigned and renamed from "Blazegrid" to "Thawline".

What makes Thawline different from other block puzzles:

1. Heat mode, a hazard system that changes the core rules. The board fights back:
   - Frozen cells lock parts of the board. Ice only cracks and melts when the player clears lines next to it.
   - Every few moves a hazard wave arrives, alternating between new ice and fire.
   - A burning block counts down and then spreads to a neighbouring block. The player must clear its line in time to put it out.
   - Fire that spreads onto ice dies out in steam, so ice becomes a tool the player can use against fire.
   - Heat mode has its own goals: thaw ice, douse fires, keep a streak.
2. A streak "temperature" that drives the whole presentation. The screen warms from ice to fire as the player keeps clearing. Big moves set off a full-screen volcano eruption (hot streak) or a glacier that freezes over and melts away (cold streak).
3. Personal touches: the player can put their own photo or one of 12 pictures on the board and reveal it as they place blocks. There are 4 block styles, 6 patterns and 3 sound packs.

How to see it quickly: on the home screen tap "Heat mode". Frozen cells are on the board from the first move, and the first fire arrives within a few moves. Clear 3 lines at once to see the volcano or glacier effect.

No account or login is needed. The app works offline. It uses Firebase Remote Config only to tune difficulty values. It has no ads and no in-app purchases.
